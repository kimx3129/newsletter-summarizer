## 7. [CopilotKit/OpenBot](https://github.com/CopilotKit/OpenBot) ✅ verified (corrected)
Source: github.com
Self-hostable, MIT-licensed workspace where each AI "coworker" gets its own workspace folder and browser profile, with full container isolation available as an opt-in toggle (default: one shared container per person); CEL-based policy gateway (fail-closed, audited before execution), audit logging with secret redaction, and human takeover for logins/2FA; ~11.6k stars/1.4k forks.
