## 1. [Introducing Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-fable-and-mythos-5-2)
**Source:** Anthropic News

Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.2 (GA) and Claude Mythos 5.2 (vetted-org access via Cyber/Life-Sciences Verification Programs) on Nov 9, 2026, built on the same underlying model. Outputs up to 30% faster than 5.1 (fast mode up to 2.5x more); pricing $8/$40 per million input/output tokens.
Fable 5.2 scores 49.1% on FrontierCode 2.0 at Xhigh effort (highest of any released model tested) and 45.3% at High effort vs. Opus 5.5's 45.5% at High effort, at roughly half the cost; 69.9% on FrontierSWE v2 vs. Opus 5.5's 62.3%. Mythos 5.2 found 10.2% more vulnerabilities than Opus 5.5 in an internal CVE-rediscovery eval (1,829 CVEs).
~1,000 Fable 5.2 agents formalized Fermat's Last Theorem in Lean in 30 hours (vs. 11 days prior); >90% of merged changes to Anthropic's own codebase are now Claude-written; safety metrics improved vs. Opus 5.5 and prior Mythos; external evaluators include UK AISI, METR, and Frontier Design.

**Why it matters:** Faster, benchmark-leading model for agentic coding/security research; gated Mythos access signals risk management of specialized models.

## 2. [meta/astryx](https://github.com/meta/astryx)
**Source:** GitHub Trending

Meta open-sourced Astryx, a beta design system on React 19+/StyleX, 150+ accessible components, theming, dark mode, templates, and a CLI (including a `swizzle` command to eject component source). 20.0k stars, 7,218 commits, MIT license; claims 8 years of internal use across 13,000+ apps; 7 theme packages.
Targets both humans and AI agents, ships AGENTS.md/CLAUDE.md; styling overridable with Tailwind/CSS modules/plain CSS.

**Why it matters:** Agent-compatible component library from Meta lowers cost of AI-assisted UI building.

## 6. [OpenBMB/VoxCPM](https://github.com/OpenBMB/VoxCPM)
**Source:** GitHub Trending

OpenBMB released VoxCPM2, a 2B-param tokenizer-free TTS model on a MiniCPM-4 backbone, trained on 2M+ hours of multilingual speech. Supports 30 languages plus Chinese dialects, 48kHz output from 16kHz reference, Seed-TTS-eval English WER 1.84%/SIM 75.3%, Chinese CER 0.97%/SIM 79.5%, RTF ~0.30 on RTX 4090 (~0.13 with Nano-vLLM), ~8GB VRAM. 42.1k stars, Apache-2.0.
Voice design from text, emotion/pace cloning from short clips, LoRA fine-tuning from 5-10 min audio; trails competitors like Qwen3-TTS on some benchmarks while leading on others.

**Why it matters:** Compact, commercially-licensed, self-hostable multilingual TTS competitive with closed systems.

## 8. [opensandbox-group/OpenSandbox](https://github.com/opensandbox-group/OpenSandbox)
**Source:** GitHub Trending

Agentic AI Foundation project, secure extensible sandbox runtime for AI agents to execute code/commands/manage files/control browsers-desktops. 16.9k stars, Apache-2.0; Firecracker-based sandbox creation ~64ms P50 (serial) and ~125.4ms P99 (10 concurrent). Pause/resume preserves memory/disk; runs on Docker, scales to Kubernetes via one API.
SDKs Python/Java-Kotlin/TypeScript/C#.NET/Go, `osb` CLI, MCP server for Claude Code/Cursor, Credential Vault, network ingress/egress policy controls; tracked gap (issue #2463) in image signing/provenance for umbrella releases.

**Why it matters:** Security boundary infrastructure determining whether autonomous agent deployments at scale are safe.
