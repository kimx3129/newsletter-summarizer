### 1. [Comfy-Org/ComfyUI](https://github.com/Comfy-Org/ComfyUI)
Source: GitHub
Open-source (GPL-3.0) node-graph GUI/API/backend for diffusion models (SD 1.5, SDXL, Flux.1, and other image/video/audio/3D models), ~139.7k stars/16.6k forks.
Includes App Mode (simplified UI for complex workflows) and ComfyUI-Manager (custom node install/update extension); core stable releases ship roughly every two weeks.
**Why it matters:** Remains the dominant open visual workflow tool for diffusion models; fast release cadence and growing feature set keep it relevant for hobbyists and production pipelines alike.

### 2. [Anthropic commits 5% of shares to new "Public Benefit Fund"](https://www.anthropic.com/news/public-benefit-fund)
Source: Anthropic
Anthropic is committing 5% of its shares (equity, not profit) to a newly created, wholly-owned LLC called the Anthropic Public Benefit Fund, pending stockholder approval. The board can add up to 2% more for every 50% increase in company valuation above a set baseline.
None of the fund's equity may be used for lobbying or political giving, in the US or abroad. Combined with co-founders' pledge to donate 80% of personal equity and an employee equity-donation match program (~half of employees participate), total earmarked reaches roughly 20% of shares.
**Why it matters:** One of the largest unilateral equity commitments to public benefit by a frontier AI lab, with an explicit lobbying/political-giving ban notable given ongoing debate over AI companies' political influence.

### 3. [stablyai/orca](https://github.com/stablyai/orca)
Source: GitHub
Orca is an open-source (MIT) cross-platform app orchestrating multiple AI coding agents (Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, Pi) in parallel, each in its own isolated git worktree. Supports fan-out of one prompt to 5 agents, remote SSH execution, iOS/Android companion apps, and a "Design Mode" turning clicked UI elements into agent prompts.
~98.3k stars/6.3k forks.
**Why it matters:** Multi-agent orchestration with isolated worktrees and remote/mobile control addresses a real pain point in parallel AI coding workflows and signals growing tooling maturity in this space.
