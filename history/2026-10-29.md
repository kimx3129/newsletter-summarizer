3. **heretic — Tool that automatically removes censorship from language models**
   Source: GitHub — https://github.com/p-e-w/heretic
   Summary: Heretic (AGPL-3.0, by Philipp Emanuel Weidmann) automatically removes safety-alignment refusals from transformer LLMs via directional ablation ("abliteration"), without retraining. Uses Optuna TPE optimizer to tune per-layer ablation params; on gemma-3-12b-it cut refusals from 97/100 to 3/100 while holding KL-divergence to 0.16, vs 1.04 and 0.45 for two prior tools. ~37.0k stars, 4.2k forks; supports dense + some MoE/hybrid architectures; `pip install heretic-llm`, ~20-30 min on RTX 3090 for a 4B model; community reportedly published 5,000+ derived models.
   Why it matters: Lowers the bar for stripping refusal behavior from open-weight models.
   Status: ✅ verified
